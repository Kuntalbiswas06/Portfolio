(02)About

A developer who enjoys understanding how things work.

I’m Kuntal Biswas, a B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering student at Narula Institute of Technology, India. I’m passionate about software engineering, backend development, problem-solving and building useful digital products.

I started with programming fundamentals, then moved into full-stack development by building real projects. Along the way the backend became the part I’m most drawn to: how data flows, how services talk to each other, and how systems are designed to scale.

I learn best by building. Projects like MaanDrishti, SwasthyaSetu and ReliefRoute are how I turn ideas about verification, healthcare access and disaster relief into working software — and how I find out what I still need to learn.
